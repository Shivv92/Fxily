Frequently Asked Questions

Is our company data secure?
Yes. Absolute security is our priority. We deploy private models and ensure that your proprietary data is never used to train public AI tools like ChatGPT. Your data stays in your secure cloud or on-premise servers.
How long does implementation take?
Implementation timelines vary based on the complexity of your business processes. A simple internal knowledge bot can take 2-4 weeks, while comprehensive workflow automation can take 2-3 months.
Do I need a technical team to use Fixly?
Not at all. We build the tools so they integrate directly into software you already use (like Slack, Teams, Salesforce, or your own website). We provide full training for your non-technical staff.
